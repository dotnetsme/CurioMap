# Vision

**CurioMap — a live map of human curiosity.**

Most reading is a private act. We think it can be a shared, curious, and kind one —
without sacrificing who you are or where you live.

CurioMap is a **global, open-source, geolocation-based reading graph**:

- **Anonymous intellectual identity** — show your mind, not your resume. Curated avatars,
  generated handles, and a real name that is always opt-in and reversible.
- **Curiosity you can see on a map** — readers near you, a fuzzed global heatmap, top books
  per region. Coarse by design: country / region / city, plus a coordinate rounded to a
  ~15 km grid. Never your exact location.
- **Short, focused conversations** — prompt-driven threads about books, with reactions
  instead of emoji spam, and quiet threads that archive themselves.
- **Built in public** — Apache 2.0, a public roadmap, regular dev logs, and an open API.
- **Financed ethically** — transparent affiliate links fund the map. No ad profiles, no
  selling data, ever.

We succeed when a reader in Berlin and a reader in Bangalore both feel they can say
*“I’m curious about this right now”* — without giving away a single thing they didn't
choose to share.
