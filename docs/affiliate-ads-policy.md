# Affiliate & ads policy

Financing CurioMap without selling you out.

- **Affiliate links**: “Buy this book” buttons are generated from the book's ISBN and a
  configurable tag. Purchasing through them pays CurioMap a commission at **no extra cost
  to you**. Links are labeled and carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"` (disclosed).
- **Ads (later)**: the MVP ships no ads. If topic-based, non-intrusive ads arrive later,
  they will be server-side controlled and never based on cross-site tracking. Layout and
  datasets are already structured for server-driven ad slots.
- **No data selling**: personal data, reading history, and location are never sold or
  shared with advertisers. Region-level aggregates (e.g., “top book in Berlin”) may be
  shared; individuals never are.
- **Transparency**: click counts and revenue are published in the dev logs, even when
  tiny. Curated override links never point anywhere outside the disclosure.
